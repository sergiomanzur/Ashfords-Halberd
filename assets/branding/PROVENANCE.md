# Original Ashford's Halberd presentation

Created for this project on 2026-10-23. No artwork or music from the reference
port or the retail game is used in these launcher assets.

`ashford-icon.png` and `launcher.png` were generated with the built-in OpenAI
image-generation tool. ICO and BMP files are format/size conversions of those
originals for Windows and SDL. The generation prompts were:

The complete submitted prompts are preserved in `prompts.txt` in this folder.

- Icon: original brass halberd heraldic silhouette within an oxblood circular
  seal over midnight ink; restrained engraving, clear small-size silhouette,
  no text, characters, existing franchise marks or watermark.
- Launcher: original secluded gothic manor entrance at night, rain and mist,
  wrought-iron gates, warm distant window and brass halberd at right; dark left
  half for menu controls; charcoal, oxblood and antique brass palette; no
  people, text, existing game artwork or watermark.

`launcher.wav` is an original deterministic synthesized ambient composition.
Its complete score and synthesis source is `tools/generate-launcher-music.py`.
It contains no sampled recording or borrowed soundtrack melody.
